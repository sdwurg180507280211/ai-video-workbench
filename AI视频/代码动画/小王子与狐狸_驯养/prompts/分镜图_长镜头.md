# 分镜图（9 个长镜头，3 张）提示词

对应剧本的「长镜头表」。每个长镜头画成一排：从左到右是这个镜头里的几个关键画面，画面之间用箭头连起来，表示镜头怎么走（平移、推近、拉远、叠化）。旧的分镜图在 `imgs/old/`。

模型：gpt-image-2.5-sunburst（本机生图网关，images/generations，quality/size auto）。每张的提示词 = 公共部分 + 那一张的三排。

## 公共部分

```
A professional animation storyboard sheet for LONG TAKES, landscape, 3 rows. Each ROW is ONE continuous long take, labeled at the far left with a small code ("L1"...). Inside a row, 4 or 5 key frames (each a 16:9 frame with a thin pencil border) are placed left to right and linked by small arrows between them; next to each arrow a tiny camera symbol shows the move: a horizontal arrow for a pan, corner arrows pointing inward for zoom-in, outward for zoom-out, a soft double circle for a dissolve. No other text, no captions, no speech bubbles.

Style: soft pencil line art with light transparent watercolor washes on warm cream paper, children's picture-book feel. Flat 2D layered scenery (sky, mid wheat field, foreground grass). No 3D perspective tricks: camera only pans, zooms and dissolves.

Characters are drawn LARGE in the frame (at least a quarter of the frame height) except in the few extreme wide shots.

Characters (keep consistent): a small boy with messy GOLDEN hair, a short jacket and dark trousers, NO scarf; a small slim fox with very big ears and a white tail tip. Screen direction: the boy is always on the LEFT, the fox on the RIGHT.

Color rule: the world starts as uncolored grey pencil; only the boy's golden hair is colored, and the red rose on his tiny planet is red. The fox is grey until it sniffs his hand (then orange). The grass turns green when the fox sits in the empty spot on day 3; the sky and the boy's jacket turn light blue while they watch the sunset. The wheat field stays grey until the climax, when it turns gold.
```

## 第 1 张：L1～L3

```
Row L1: (1) close shot in the grass: a fox tail glowing ORANGE in an all-grey pencil world, half of a grey fox face peeking out. (2) zoom-out and pan left: a whole grey wheat sea, a wind wave rolling from right to left. (3) the boy (only golden hair colored), drawn large, walks in from the left, then sits looking up to the upper left. (4) pan upward to a GREY pencil dusk sky (no blue yet) with a tiny star at upper left, zoom-in arrow. (5) dissolve: a tiny planet with a red rose under a glass dome.
Row L2: (1) medium: the boy sits hugging his knees and pats the ground beside him. (2) pan right at grass height: two grey fox ears rising from the grass, eyes looking left at him. (3) low camera at the fox's height: big grey stranger's boots stomp across the path from left to right, the grey fox ducking into the grass. (4) the boots gone, the fox peeking out again; a water drop spreading on paper as a transition mark.
Row L3 (one fixed frame repeated, low camera, the camera never moves except small zooms; both characters drawn LARGE in the lower half of the frame, the distance between them shown by empty space, not by making them small): (1) day 1: the boy on the left, the grey fox far on the right, a small sun-moon arc above. (2) day 2: zoom-in between them: his hand stopped in mid-air, the fox sniffing it and turning ORANGE from the nose. (3) day 3: the boy alone beside an empty patch, then the orange fox sitting in it with GREEN spreading from its paws. (4) the two side by side at sunset, sky and his jacket light BLUE, wheat still grey.
```

## 第 2 张：L4～L6

```
Row L4: (1) medium: afternoon, the orange fox waiting alone at the same spot, tail sweeping, sun above a treetop, green grass, blue sky, grey wheat. (2) zoom-in: one fox ear turning left. (3) pan left: a tuft of golden hair appearing over the grass line. (4) zoom-out and track: the fox leaps from the right and runs a short way left, stopping just to the right of the boy.
Row L5: (1) medium side view: the two sitting together, wind blowing his golden hair and the grey wheat in the same direction, the fox looking at his hair. (2) pan following the fox's gaze to the grey wheat field. (3) zoom-in: a strand of golden hair dissolving into the shape of a wheat ear, the tip flashing a tiny spark of gold. (4) pan upward to the evening sky.
Row L6: (1) dissolve: on the tiny planet the wind has knocked the glass dome over and the uncovered red rose bends. (2) dissolve back: the boy at dusk looking up to the upper left, brows knitted. (3) pan down to fox height: only the boy's legs and jacket hem as he stands; the fox's tail curled around the hem. (4) the boy crouches in from the left, eye level with the fox. (5) zoom-in, side view at hand height: the boy's two hands cupped together palms up, the fox's nose resting in them.
```

## 第 3 张：L7～L9

```
Row L7 (the climax): (1) extreme close-up of the fox's eye with a tiny reflection of golden hair. (2) pan down following its gaze to a small patch of flattened GREEN grass where they sat, surrounded by still-grey wheat ears. (3) zoom-out: GOLD seeping outward from beside that patch through the wheat ears like watercolor bleeding into paper, a faint ghost image of the two watching a sunset in the waves. (4) the whole wheat field golden, still.
From here on the whole wheat field is GOLDEN in every panel (it turned gold in L7 and stays gold). Row L8: (1) the fox drawn large in the right foreground, seen from behind-right, half of its face visible; far away on the left the boy has stopped, turned back and is waving; the fox's tail raised; the field is golden under a dusk sky. (2) slow zoom-in: the fox looking down at the empty patch of green grass beside it, ears drooping; golden wheat all around. (3) the fox lifting its head toward the golden waves, ears rising. (4) close-up of its side face: eyes closed, a gentle smile.
Row L9: (1) pan up to a starry night sky. (2) dissolve. SIDE VIEW with the camera at hand height (NOT a front view), the SAME composition and hand shape as the cupped hands in L6 (5): the boy's two hands cupped together PALMS UP, gently cradling the bottom rim of the glass dome where it meets the ground of his tiny planet; the red rose inside. (3) dissolve back: the golden wheat field with the small fox sitting beside the empty patch, slight zoom-out, an empty area at the top for the title.
```
